Devices

Cameras and microphones on this computer

Built-in, USB, and connected Continuity devices that macOS already sees. Connect the device in macOS first, then click Refresh. Names appear after you grant access once.

"Grant access" opens the camera and microphone for a moment so names show, then stops them. Nothing is streamed.

Phone or another computer on the same Wi-Fi

Browsers only allow camera and microphone access on a secure page. On this Mac, plain http://127.0.0.1:8081/ works. From another device the page must be served over HTTPS with a certificate that the other device trusts.

Each device runs the Faces and Speech pages independently: its own camera or microphone, its own matching state, and its own Whisper connection. The face model on this Mac serves them one frame at a time, and the enrolled gallery is shared.
