KAWK ambient memory

Just talk and look around. After Start, three timestamped streams are saved continuously: photos (one every 5 s), speech and face identities. There is no wake word and nothing to send: everything is kept, and KAWK decides on its own when assistance is useful and posts it under Updates from KAWK automatically. Memory shows everything saved. Nothing records until you press Start.
